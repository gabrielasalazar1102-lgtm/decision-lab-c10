How are these statistics calculated?

EMV: multiply each payoff by its state's probability, then add. Max Payoff: the largest payoff for that alternative. Min Payoff: its smallest payoff. Regret: the best payoff among all original alternatives in a state minus your alternative's payoff. Max Regret: the largest of its state regrets. These benchmarks remain fixed during staged filtering.

03 · Criterion decisions

Compare the four decision criteria

Each criterion evaluates the complete original set independently. Different criteria may recommend different alternatives. Ties are identified using an absolute tolerance of 10⁻⁹.

Maximax: choose the largest maximum payoff. Maximin: choose the largest minimum payoff. Minimax Regret: choose the smallest maximum regret. EMV/Bayes: choose the largest probability-weighted expected payoff.
04 · Staged filtering

Apply decision rules in sequence

Each stage evaluates only the alternatives that survived the preceding stage. Eliminated alternatives never re-enter. All alternatives tied at the cutoff survive, in their original input order.
